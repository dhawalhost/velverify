# India DPDP Addendum

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 15 March 2026

This Addendum supplements the WardSeal Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and Data Processing Addendum for personal data processing connected to India.

If there is a conflict between this Addendum and the baseline policy set, this Addendum controls for India-linked processing.

## 1. Scope and statutory framing

This Addendum is intended to support compliance alignment with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP), and applicable rules as notified.

## 2. Role language

For India-linked processing:

- WardSeal may act as Data Fiduciary for account, billing, and business contact data it determines purposes for.
- WardSeal acts as Processor/Data Processor for customer-controlled end-user identity data processed on customer instructions.

## 3. Notice and lawful processing

WardSeal provides notice of processing purposes and categories through the baseline Privacy Policy and associated product notices.

Where consent is used as the lawful basis, consent can be withdrawn, subject to legal and contractual constraints and technical feasibility.

## 4. Data Principal rights and grievance redressal

Subject to applicable law, individuals may request:

- Access-related information about personal data processing
- Correction and updating of personal data
- Erasure of personal data
- Grievance redressal
- Nomination rights

Requests and grievances can be raised at: privacy@wardseal.com

WardSeal may verify request identity before processing requests.

## 5. Cross-border transfer handling

WardSeal applies contractual and technical safeguards for cross-border transfers, and will adapt controls to any country-specific transfer restrictions notified under Indian law.

## 6. Children and guardianship handling

WardSeal services are intended for enterprise use and are not directed at children. Where customer use cases involve minors, customers are responsible for obtaining any legally required parental/guardian authorizations and controls.

## 7. Security and incident management

Security controls and incident response commitments are defined in:

- `docs/policies/security-and-trust.md`
- `docs/policies/incident-response-and-breach-notification-policy.md`

WardSeal notifies affected customers of confirmed reportable incidents without undue delay, as required by law or contract.

## 8. Redline minimization notes

This Addendum is drafted to avoid structural rewrites of baseline policy documents; it adds India-specific legal framing and rights language only.
