# EU/EEA Privacy and DPA Addendum

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 15 March 2026

This Addendum supplements the WardSeal Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and Data Processing Addendum for customers and data subjects in the European Union and European Economic Area.

If there is a conflict between this Addendum and the baseline policy set, this Addendum controls for EU/EEA processing.

## 1. Scope and roles

For EU/EEA personal data:

- WardSeal acts as **Controller** for account, billing, and business contact data.
- WardSeal acts as **Processor** (or sub-processor, where applicable) for customer-managed end-user identity data.

## 2. GDPR legal bases

Where GDPR applies, processing is based on one or more of:

- Article 6(1)(b): performance of a contract
- Article 6(1)(c): legal obligation
- Article 6(1)(f): legitimate interests (including security, abuse prevention, and service reliability)
- Article 6(1)(a): consent, where required

## 3. Data subject rights

Data subjects may request:

- Access
- Rectification
- Erasure
- Restriction
- Objection
- Portability

Requests may be submitted to privacy@wardseal.com. WardSeal may verify identity before fulfilling requests.

## 4. Cross-border transfers

Where EU/EEA personal data is transferred outside the EU/EEA, WardSeal applies:

- The current EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), where required
- Supplementary technical and organizational measures appropriate to transfer risk

## 5. Subprocessors

Current subprocessor categories and regions are listed in:

- `docs/policies/subprocessors-and-international-transfers.md`

WardSeal provides notice of material subprocessor changes where required by agreement.

## 6. Security and incident handling

WardSeal applies technical and organizational measures described in:

- `docs/policies/security-and-trust.md`
- `docs/policies/incident-response-and-breach-notification-policy.md`

For GDPR-reportable incidents involving processor-handled personal data, WardSeal notifies affected customers without undue delay after confirmation.

## 7. Supervisory authority complaints

Data subjects have the right to lodge a complaint with their competent supervisory authority.

## 8. EU representative and DPO

Where legally required, WardSeal designates and publishes:

- EU Representative: privacy@wardseal.com
- Data Protection Officer (if required): dpo@wardseal.com

## 9. Redline minimization notes

This Addendum is designed as a lightweight overlay. No baseline policy clauses are deleted; this document only clarifies GDPR-specific obligations and controls.
