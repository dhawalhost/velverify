# U.S. State Privacy Addendum

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 15 March 2026

This Addendum supplements the WardSeal Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and Data Processing Addendum for U.S. state privacy law compliance (including CCPA/CPRA-style requirements where applicable).

If there is a conflict between this Addendum and the baseline policy set, this Addendum controls for covered U.S. state processing.

## 1. Scope

This Addendum applies where U.S. state privacy laws grant rights or impose obligations relevant to WardSeal’s services and customer use.

## 2. Business role and service provider/processor posture

Depending on context:

- WardSeal acts as a business/controller for account, billing, and commercial relationship data.
- WardSeal acts as service provider/contractor/processor (as applicable) for customer-provided end-user identity data processed to provide services.

## 3. Consumer privacy rights support

Where applicable law requires, WardSeal supports workflows for:

- Right to know/access
- Right to correct
- Right to delete
- Right to data portability
- Appeal process (where required)

Requests may be submitted at privacy@wardseal.com or https://help.wardseal.com.

## 4. Sale/share and targeted advertising statement

WardSeal does not sell personal data for monetary consideration.

If a specific deployment uses cross-context behavioral advertising or other regulated sharing activities, customers must configure and publish required notices and opt-out mechanisms.

## 5. Sensitive personal information

WardSeal processes sensitive identity/security-related fields strictly for service delivery, security operations, fraud prevention, and legal compliance, as defined in contract and customer instructions.

## 6. Non-discrimination

WardSeal does not discriminate against individuals for exercising privacy rights, subject to legal exceptions.

## 7. Contractual flow-down terms

For covered U.S. state laws, customer agreements may include service provider/processor clauses restricting:

- Retention, use, or disclosure outside contract purposes
- Data combining except as legally permitted
- Secondary use incompatible with contractual instructions

## 8. Security and incident handling

Security and incident commitments are documented in:

- `docs/policies/security-and-trust.md`
- `docs/policies/incident-response-and-breach-notification-policy.md`

## 9. Redline minimization notes

This Addendum is intentionally concise and additive, minimizing edits needed to baseline policy files while enabling U.S. state privacy disclosures.
