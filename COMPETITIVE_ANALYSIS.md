## 🚀 The "Identity Frontier": 3 Pillars of Greatness

To become the **leading** platform, WardSeal must dominate these three emerging frontiers that incumbents are currently struggling with:

### 1. Non-Human Identity (NHI) Management
Most platforms are built for humans. In 2026, machine IDs (bots, AI agents, service accounts) outnumber humans 50:1.
- **Greatness Gap**: Implementing a first-class "Agent Identity" lifecycle. WardSeal should be the first platform to manage the **Access Requests of AI Agents** via the same ChatOps flow used for humans.
- **Wedge**: Identity-as-a-Service for LLM-driven workflows.

### 2. Autonomous Risk & Adaptive Flow
Static policies are no longer enough.
- **Greatness Gap**: An "Identity Risk Engine" that continuously monitors behavior (IP drift, off-hours, anomalous interaction patterns).
- **Wedge**: When a risk is detected, WardSeal sends an **Interactive Risk Alert** to the user or manager in Slack: *"Your session was flagged for high risk. Re-verify via Passkeys now?"*

### 3. Converged "Single Source of Truth"
The market is tired of separate AM and IGA tools.
- **Greatness Gap**: A truly converged "Identity Graph" where **Authentication, Authorization, and Governance** share the same data model (Zanzibar-based).
- **Wedge**: A visual "Relationship Explorer" that shows exactly *why* someone has access across multiple apps, visually.
